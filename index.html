Shangi Facts

The Variety at a Glance

Skin & Flesh

Cream skin with shallow pink eyes. Creamy white flesh with high dry matter — ideal for mashing and chipping.

Maturity

Around 90 days (early maturing). Very short dormancy, which means it moves quickly from farm to your kitchen.

Best Uses

Boiling, mashing, roasting, chipping and crisping. Kenya's most versatile and most loved potato variety.

Typical Yield

30–40 tonnes per hectare under good management — a strong, reliable producer for Nyandarua farmers.

Growing Altitude

Thrives between 1,500 and 3,000 metres above sea level — exactly the range of the Nyandarua highlands.

Why Farmers Love It

High market demand, short dormancy, and consistent yields make Shangi the backbone of Kenyan potato farming.
